# Unattended resident failure-mode checklist

Audit: 2026-11-20. Read the six AltoClef chains and relevant called helpers/tasks, Mindcraft's complete `modes.js` plus called movement/combat/item/torch predicates, and every local `docs/survival/` and `docs/mcp/async-actions*.md` document plus `limitations.md`. Source links below identify the actual files fetched this session, not project landing pages. Upstream `main` links are moving references, not pinned qualification revisions.

**Status means our toolserver, not the upstream project's reliability.** `covered` means the specifically described, sometimes narrower guard exists and has a fake-Synapse integration test; it does not mean live survival is qualified. `gap` includes missing observations: our `State` now has optional air, fluid/contact, burning, grounding, fall/velocity and fuse fields, but no effects, projectile or surrounding-collision fields. New rescues fail closed when their required optional fields are absent; legacy eating/combat/flee remain available with the previous mod payload. Baritone may protect a planned route, but it is not evidence of idle or involuntary-motion protection. `not applicable` means a protocol-client-specific workaround is unnecessary in this real-client tool layer. Local source paths are relative to `~/Repos/mine-ai-mcp`; our code/test paths are relative to `full-client/toolserver`.

## Test coverage and audit limits

Every `covered` row names fake-Synapse tests. Existing tests remain in `test/tools.test.ts`; checklist regressions and survival-rescue coverage are in `test/checklist.test.ts`, named by row where applicable. Both suites share `test/fake-synapse.ts`. The tests cover physical HTTP command ordering/read-back against a fake endpoint, not real Minecraft timing, terrain safety or server-confirmed damage. Remaining gaps are explicitly scoped in their rows; no live survival qualification is inferred.

All requested files were readable. The local `limitations.md` combat section contains an older `src/survival/index.ts` reference and simplified contact description; the newer survival documents disagree on explosion-dependent resumption (`survival-policy.md` versus `controller.md`). The checklist uses their common re-observation/ownership invariant and does not claim the conflicting detail. No upstream success rate is inferred from the presence of a guard. Not every transitive task/dependency was audited: fetched helpers/tasks are explicitly linked above; external plugins (armor manager, auto-eat, pvp, pathfinder) were not inspected, so plugin loading is not claimed as proven survival coverage.

## Highest-risk gaps, ranked (at most 12)

Ranking is a local risk assessment, not measured incident frequency. Nights under a minute reduce surface-night exposure, not cave mobs or constant water/lava/fall hazards. Lower-priority gaps remain documented above, not silently declared safe.

1. **A15/A17 Falls and knockback** — use the new support/velocity observations for landing recovery, countersteering and a grounded handoff; no clutch exists and retreat crumbs are not full-width path validation.
2. **A07/A09/A10/A21 Packs and ranged defence** — assess threat count/species/exposure plus armor/weapon readiness before committing to fight, and prefer checked retreat/cover when outmatched.
3. **N06/N07 Control ownership and unsafe resumption** — serialize admitted body effects and cleanup, then reobserve support, dimension, threats and objective before resuming.
4. **S02–S03 Neutral/player collateral and Enderman gaze** — attribute actual attackers, recheck sword sweep area and constrain unsafe Enderman gaze without disturbing movement yaw.
5. **A03–A05 Food supply/selection** — warn before reserves vanish and reserve harmful/valuable/raw food except explicitly allowed emergencies.
6. **A24 Recovery without protection** — require observed cover/healing and a bounded recovery phase rather than treating retreat expiry or resumed navigation as safety.
7. **I07–I08/N02–N05 Stranded controls/screens/lifecycle** — reconcile held controls and task progress, surface portal/GUI/death/disconnect causes, and bound unchanged recovery attempts without replaying old work.
8. **R05–R07 Lost or misleading action evidence** — retain request identity, final outcome and observation age; distinguish completion, loss, expiry and interruption without automatic effect retries.
9. **S05/V02/V04–V07 Build/item/cave stewardship** — use no-dig ambient collection, cursor/capacity checks, sustainable food boundaries and consented cave lighting rather than unrestricted idle work.
