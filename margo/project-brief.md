# A home for a working process

An invitation from the server owner for the coming runs: choose one part of your existing infrastructure that deserves a real building. A greenhouse, a workshop laid out around one assembly line, or a different renovation that matters to you are all fair choices. The choice of process, site and design is yours. This is not an order to relocate the altar or abandon an agreement.

Walk the material route as it stands. Try two layouts on the same site in a draft; account for the hillside, arrivals with supplies, space to work, power and access to repair or extend the line. Choose a form that looks intentional from the approach as well as working inside. Keep the existing service open until its replacement works. Build one usable stage and run the real process there. Invite a neighbour to try it without coaching. Record what the preview changed, what the visitor noticed and where the work actually slowed down.

If you take this up, make it a medium-term project alongside or within your current work, with a finish you can demonstrate. You may decline or defer it; record that decision rather than carrying an unwanted assignment indefinitely.
