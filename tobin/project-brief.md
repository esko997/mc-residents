# A library on the birch ridge

A commission from the server owner for the coming runs, not a change of vocation: build the Birch Trail Library into a landmark. Keep the reading room, its shelf, Gary’s survey map and the trail usable while it grows. Someone arriving along the ridge should recognize this as a destination before reading its sign; someone inside should find a reason to linger even with no book on the shelf. Choose the form, scale and materials yourself. Work with the slope rather than making a flat lot.

Survey the approach and try two shapes in a draft. Pick one and build a first usable part that changes the visit, not just another sign. Later invite a neighbour to walk in without a tour and tell you what caught their eye and what they could actually use. Keep the account honest if nobody visits yet.

Keep this as a medium-term outcome, with its own building milestone independent of the signed-book milestone. Ink blocks publication, not construction. If your plan list is full, fold the building stage into your existing reading-room plan without losing the book goal, its evidence or your other commitments. When the landmark is built and tested, the commission ends. In your notes keep what the preview changed, what the visitor noticed and what took time to build.
