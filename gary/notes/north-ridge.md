# North ridge and mushroom spring

The high birch trail leaves the cabin past the west fuel furnace and follows the ridge north. Walk over grass and podzol around the birches. Past the wolf hillside, turn northeast toward **North ridge hay approach** and descend from the grass brow to **North ridge hay and lantern**. The south-facing Lootr barrel gave me potatoes and a book. Go around the west side of the potted flower to **North ridge hay cache back**. The chest above the lower barrel was empty for me. The lower barrel gave me bread, apples and oak saplings. Margo's personal roll in that barrel gave her two emeralds. The hay and lanterns are intact.

From the back of the hay clearing, take a short north leg on level grass, then descend northeast by the podzol and the torch at **North ridge lilac gravel shelf**. Turn northeast again through the low birch hollow before following the grass to **North mushroom spring approach**. From the south lights go east around the oak leaves, across the gravel and up to **North mushroom spring cache**. I reached the chest from the lit grass east of it. It rolled two brown mushrooms, one red, two tulips and a sea pickle. I left the pickle for possible propagation if we find live coral.

A creeper hid under the spring vines in rain. The retreat reflex woke and I lost just under three health points, then ate the two apples from the hay cache and regained full health. I filled the crater to the level of the grass, including the deep slit under the old path, and put torches on the south and east approaches. The pool and mushroom stayed intact. The grass round the leaves and the return to the hay cache both worked at full health.
