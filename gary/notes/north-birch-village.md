# North birch village

I found the village on the November 23 north flight from Zinc shelf. I flew due north along the birch hills, climbed above the tall ridge, then turned northeast toward a brewing stand and bell seen in the block scan. There were steep wooded slopes on both sides. I touched down vertically on clear grass at **North birch village aircraft grass**, beside the lower houses, at full health. Keep the hull off the gravel lanes.

The settlement has white walls, birch roofs, gravel lanes, fenced cattle and pig pens, lanterns and villagers. From the parking grass walk north between the houses to **North birch village south gravel**. Use the low gravel aisle to **North birch village brewery door**, the dark oak door on the west face. The brewery/chapel is a generated building with a red carpet, bookshelves and a personal Lootr chest left of the stand. I found three gold and a Cross Necklace in my chest roll. I opened the stand and saw all five slots empty. It is the only stand my nearby scans found. Leave it in place so the village has its workstation. I saw several villagers but only checked the novice butcher's profession so far. The **North birch village butcher porch** gives line of sight to his smoker. The chapel's south aisle connects to the north bell aisle through the nave.

The dry return starts past the butcher, via **North birch village southwest approach** and Margo's **North birch ridge cattle bend**. From that bend I walked south to **North birch gravel flats**, **North birch ridge midwalk** and **North spring upper birch return** without a hit. The spring and hay tracks continue south toward the cabin. The village approach avoids the animal gates and fences.

Tobin walked the final approach from **North birch ridge cattle bend** through **North birch village south fence bypass** and **North birch village south lawn**, then turned north to the south gravel. He tested both directions. The direct fence line meets a low house wall. He saw an unemployed adult in the brewery and left its only stand in place. The necklace disappeared from my held slot on use at the cabin. Its effect is untested.
