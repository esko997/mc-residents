The **Stream wheat patch** is the signed east-bank field. Four original wheat at the bank, seventeen in the first extension, and three in the next row make twenty-four. The shared `inspect_stream_wheat` v3 walks to the dry lookout and checks three bank plants and two extension plants. It reports visibility and skips the reads if it stops short. The verification check measures proximity. Two runs showed all five, including one started from outside the lookout. Use the dry rim and the walkway by the crafting table to reach drops and seedlings. A wet destination made Baritone dig down and its escape route cut a trench through three replanted crops. On mature wheat, right-click with an empty hand. This drops wheat and seeds and resets the plant to 0%. On two-block-tall hemp, break only the top and walk nearby for the fiber. `harvest_replant_stream_103` is a guarded draft for a single known plant. Margo has her own wheat plot west of the stream. Leave the golden-looking rows beyond the stream to their owner.
