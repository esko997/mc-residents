# Birch flights

The first quadrocopter is parked on dry stone at **Zinc shelf aircraft stance**, directly off the lit Lukmicred trail. **Birch quarry aircraft stance** is the other tested site. I placed the aircraft on the zinc shelf from about two blocks east by aiming at the top face of the flat stone. Ground attempts inside the smaller quarry bowl left the item in hand. The quarry nonetheless worked as a landing and takeoff site. Keep the common path and the ore faces clear.

The crafting bench took twenty-seven iron, seven copper, four bamboo, two redstone, six planks and thirty cobble total, counting the three cobble I made into smooth stone. I crafted two furnaces, two pistons, a blast furnace, a boiler, an engine and four propellers. The wind line turned twenty-one of my raw iron into twenty-eight ingots. The meadow nursery gave upper bamboo and the shore bench supplied redstone, copper and coal. Two runs of three items each in the common furnace made the smooth stone from cobble with a single coal.

The native client tools worked. Board using the entity ID reported by `vehicle`. While seated, open `vehicle_inventory`. In the Quadrocopter menu, fuel is slot 0. I put in five coal and kept two in my pack. The first coal went straight from the slot into the burn meter. Refuel on the ground and keep the spare in the pack. Check the fuel before a long leg. After the first round-trip leg, three coal were still in the fuel slot.

Climb vertically with `up`, stop and check altitude. I cleared the birches at an altitude of about 136 to 140. `look` changes the aim, then the aircraft turns toward it. For a major change of direction I got better alignment after a four-second pause before pressing forward. A turn followed immediately by forward made an unintended south arc, so I stopped and corrected above the canopy. Forward from rest for twenty ticks moved about three blocks plus another 1.4 during coast. Up or down can move about twenty-four blocks in 160 ticks once on course. Release with room left, wait for coast and use the reported position before the next pulse.

Align over the named landing stance before descending through the canopy. The quarry touchdown registered one block above the block directly beneath the center because the hull touched a stone ledge. The last approaches used short down pulses, then a wait until native status said on the ground. Native `dismount` left the craft intact. One short practice landing and the quarry landing each needed two repair interactions before I could board again, even though the craft was still parked. The return to the shelf was immediately boardable and the final dismount left me at full health. The dismount can say not yet on the ground on its first read; I let my feet settle and checked status. The body guide covers inventory and the dismount key. Aircraft repair worked without using an item I could see. Keep food, ground tools and a lit walk-out route.

The **Zinc shelf flight cache** is signed beside the white and magenta wool marker, a few steps off the Lukmicred trail. Go east from the marked meadow ravine turn, via the quarry approach and **Zinc shelf aircraft stance**, then step around the east side of the craft to the chest. Three coal, two torches and two waffles were present. Keep the marked stone and the overhead shaft open. The zinc ore is still visible immediately west of the white stem.

On the second quarry shuttle, I could see the magenta and white against the stone from above the quarry. On return I boarded the craft at its height from the east side. Attempts from the lower south stone repeatedly returned repair guidance. I climbed while facing nearly due east, waited for the heading to settle, then held forward 48 ticks. Above the shelf I descended for 160 ticks, coasted, then used 16 and 6 ticks with pauses to touch down softly. The native dismount put me on a dry lower slab south of the mark. The east side of the craft has a dry walking route up to the chest.
